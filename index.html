About Us

Helpers Dynasty is a health-focused organization dedicated to developing compassionate minds who extend kindness, build community resilience, foster friendship and mutual understanding.

Our Structure

The group is structured into several committees, each responsible for specific functions.
Public Health Education

Leads the development and delivery of public health education through social media campaigns, community health talks, and interactive Q&A sessions.

Health Education

Coordinates study live sessions, monthly community hangouts, peer-to-peer support, and research projects.

Social Media Management

Oversees the organization’s digital presence, ensuring consistent, ethical, and engaging communication across all social media platforms.

Publicity

To promote the group’s mission, activities, and achievements to the wider community through offline publicity, interpersonal communication, networking, and public relations - without relying on social media.

Members Welfare

Supports the wellbeing of members by fostering a safe, inclusive, and responsive community environment.
