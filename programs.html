Our Programs

Public Health Campaigns

We run awareness campaigns on key public health issues using simple, relatable messages that people can apply in their daily lives.

Community Engagement

We organize interactive talks, Q&A sessions, and outreach programs. A highlight of our calendar is our annual elderly outreach visit, where we present gifts and spend time learning from our elders.

Monthly Unifier

Our flagship monthly series featuring public health case scenarios designed for learning, discussion, and practical application.

All scenarios are fictional and used strictly for public health education purposes.
